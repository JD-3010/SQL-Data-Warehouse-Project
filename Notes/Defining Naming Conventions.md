What is Naming Conventions ?
**Set of Rules or guidelines for naming anything in the project**, Whether is a .,

- Database
- Schema
- Tables
- Store Procedures

Why should we use Naming Conventions ?
While doing a project , data or tables will be passed from one team to team or person to person . But they might be using naming conventions which is comfortable to them . 

**Hence as a best practice and to avoid any confusions , we will be setting a basic rule that every one should a particular naming convention for the entire project.**

What are the types of Naming Conventions :
![[Pasted image 20260210153511.png]]
1. Screaming Snake Case : Every letter in uppercase separated by underscore( _ ) . For Eg : SCREAMING_SNAKE_CASE

2. Camel Case : 1st word will start in lowercase and 2nd word will start in uppercase . For Eg : camelCase

3. Kebab Case : words are separated by dash ( - ) . For eg : kebab-case

4. Snake Case : All letters are in lowercase and is separated by underscore( _ ). For Eg : snake_case

5. Pascal Case : All the word's first letter will be in uppercase . For eg : UpperCamel
